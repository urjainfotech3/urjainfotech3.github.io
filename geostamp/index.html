GeoStamp

Business · For solar installers, contractors and field teams

A camera that prints the address, GPS location, date and time on every photo, and saves the location inside the photo file too. Made for site proof. No login.

What it does

  • Live camera with the address and GPS shown before you shoot
  • 3 stamp styles: Classic, Compact and Site Report
  • Change the stamp size, darkness and position (top or bottom)
  • Photo shape 4:3 or 16:9, in Standard or High quality
  • Pinch to zoom, tap to focus
  • Your company logo, project name and a default note on every stamp (My stamp)
  • Photos saved in Gallery › Pictures/GeoStamp, with a separate folder for each project
  • Option to also keep the original photo without a stamp
  • Location also saved inside the photo file (EXIF), so Google Photos and Maps can read it
  • Each photo gets a unique ID (GS-XXXXXXXX)
  • Verify a photo: checks whether a photo is unchanged since GeoStamp saved it (on the phone that took it)
  • Reports: today's photos as a PDF, and all photo locations as CSV (Excel) or KML (Google Earth)

Built for trust

  • A warning is printed on the photo if a fake-GPS app is used
  • A warning if the phone clock is wrong, with GPS time printed instead
  • If the location is not fresh, the stamp says how old it is
  • Never loses a photo: if stamping fails, the photo is still saved

Works in the field

  • Coordinates work without internet. The address is added to photos taken while online.
  • Light app (under 1 MB) that runs on low-cost phones
  • Android 10 or newer
  • No login, no account

Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos
  • Location: only while the app is open, never in the background
  • Internet: only to look up the address for a location
  • No storage permission needed

Your privacy

  • Photos, project details and the photo log stay on your phone
  • To show an address, only the GPS coordinates are sent to an address service
  • No ads and no account. The app has no analytics.
