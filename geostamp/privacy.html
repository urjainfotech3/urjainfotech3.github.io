GeoStamp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short

  • Your photos, project details and photo log stay on your phone.
  • To show an address, only the GPS coordinates are sent to an address service. These services also see the phone's internet (IP) address, as with any online request.
  • No ads, no account and no login. The app has no analytics.
  • Location is used only while the app is open, never in the background.

1. Who we are

GeoStamp is made by Urja Infotech, an independent developer in Kachchh, Gujarat, India. You can reach us at urjainfotech3@gmail.com.

2. Information the app stores on your phone

GeoStamp keeps the following only on your phone:

  • The photos you take, saved in Gallery › Pictures/GeoStamp, with a folder for each project. If you choose, the original photo without a stamp is kept too.
  • Your stamp settings: company logo, project names and default note.
  • A photo log used for reports and for checking photos with "Verify a photo".

We do not receive any of this. App data is not backed up to the cloud. If you uninstall the app, its settings and photo log are deleted. Photos already saved in your Gallery stay there until you delete them.

3. Camera

The camera is used only to take photos when you press the shutter.

4. Location

GeoStamp uses your location only while the app is open, to print the address, GPS coordinates and accuracy on each photo. It never uses location in the background.

The location is also saved inside each photo file (EXIF data), so apps like Google Photos and Google Maps can show where it was taken. If you share a photo, the location inside it goes with it, as well as the location printed on the stamp.

5. Address lookup

To turn GPS coordinates into an address, the app sends only the coordinates to:

  • your phone's built-in address service, provided by Google, and
  • OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.

No photo, name, project detail or device identifier is sent. These services also see the phone's internet (IP) address, as with any online request. These services handle the request under their own privacy policies. Address data © OpenStreetMap contributors. Coordinates work without internet. The address is added to photos taken while online; photos taken offline keep the coordinates only.

6. Internet access

The app uses the internet only for the address lookup described above.

7. No ads, no account, no analytics in the app

GeoStamp shows no ads and has no account or login. The app has no analytics or tracking code.

8. Reports and sharing

PDF, CSV and KML reports are created on your phone. They leave the phone only if you choose to share or save them somewhere else.

9. Children

GeoStamp is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

10. Data security and deletion

Because your data stays on your phone, you control it. You can delete photos from your Gallery at any time, and uninstalling the app removes its settings and photo log.

11. This website

This website (urjainfotech3.github.io) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits and see which links bring visitors. It does not use cookies, does not store anything on your device and does not identify individual visitors. The GeoStamp app itself does not use it.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

13. Contact
