The asura armies are marching on India's temples. Raise towers, choose divine blessings and blow the Shankh to protect the dhvaja. A 3D tower defence journey across Bharat, painted in folk art.

  • 🛕 3 chapters, 14 temples: Maharashtra (Warli), Mithila (Madhubani), Rajasthan (miniature art), each with its own temple
  • 👹 Boss battles: Mahishasura, Kaliya and Raktabeej
  • 🏹 5 divine towers: Dhanurdhar, Agni Kund, Vayu Stambh, Matsya Fountain, Surya Darpan
  • ✨ Blessings between waves, 3 stars per temple and Kathin mode
  • 🪷 Earn Punya for permanent blessings, tower looks and temple flags
  • 📴 Plays fully offline, no login, your progress stays on your phone
