Raksha: Temple Defence - Privacy Policy

Developer: Urja Infotech, Kachchh, Gujarat, India  ·  Contact: urjainfotech3@gmail.com  ·  Last updated: 24 November 2026

What the game stores

Your progress (stars, Punya, settings, purchases) is saved only on your phone. We do not run servers, create accounts or collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: videos you choose to watch for rewards, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalised ads, using Google's consent tool; you can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy & ads. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases (Remove ads, Starter pack) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is designed for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's Google settings. Uninstalling the game deletes all data stored on your phone.
