How you play

Read the bowler. Time your tap.

  1. 1

    Aim your shot

    Tap left, centre or right to go leg side, straight or off side.

  2. 2

    Tap for safe, swipe up to loft

    A tap keeps it on the ground. Swipe up to go over the fielders, but they can catch it.

  3. 3

    Find the gaps

    A live field map shows the open gaps in yellow along the rope.
